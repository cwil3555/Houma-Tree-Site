What moves the price

Six things decide what a tree job costs. When you call, these are what we’re sizing up.

01

Size

Height and trunk diameter are the biggest levers. A 60-foot live oak is many times the work of a 20-foot crepe myrtle.

02

Access

A tree a truck can back up to is cheaper than one behind a house that has to be roped and carried out piece by piece.

03

What’s underneath

A tree over open lawn is straightforward. One leaning over a roof, a fence, or a power line needs careful rigging, which costs more.

04

Condition

Dead, rotted, or storm-damaged trees are less predictable and more dangerous to take down, which can raise the price.

05

Cleanup

Full haul-off costs more than leaving the wood cut to length. Stump grinding and debris removal add to the base price.

06

Timing

Scheduled work is cheaper than a 2am storm emergency. After a hurricane, demand across the parish is at its peak.

Worth knowing

Storm damage and your insurance

When a tree falls on a covered structure — a roof, a garage, a fence — homeowner’s insurance often helps with removal and repair. We can’t speak for your policy, but we document emergency work with photos so you have what you need to file a claim. Check your coverage, and keep the paperwork.

Pricing questions

What is the average cost of tree removal in Houma?

For a typical residential tree, most Houma-area removals land around $500, with a common range of $300 to $1,500. Small trees can be less; large live oaks near a house, or anything needing a crane, run higher.

Why is emergency tree removal more expensive?

A tree that has already fallen on a roof or a power line is under load and far more dangerous to cut than a standing tree. That risk, plus after-hours response, is why storm and emergency calls average around $794 rather than the standard removal price.

Does the price include stump grinding?

Not by default — stump grinding uses different equipment and is priced separately, usually $166 to $307 per stump. It’s cheaper to bundle grinding with the removal than to book a second trip.

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. Every estimate is free and on-site, because the price depends on things you can only judge in person — the tree’s size, what’s underneath it, and how easily we can reach it.
