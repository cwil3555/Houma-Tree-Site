Why it matters here

Bayou trees are a different job

Removing a tree in South Louisiana isn’t like doing it on dry, open ground up north. The soil stays soft and waterlogged, lots run narrow, and big spreading canopies grow right up against houses and power lines. That’s before a single storm rolls off the Gulf.

We work these conditions every week — the brittle water oaks that shed limbs, the live oaks that need real rope work, the bald cypress and water tupelo down in the wet. Knowing the local trees and the local ground is half the job.

  • Live oakHeavy, wide canopies close to homes. Careful rigging, not straight felling.
  • Water oakFast-growing and brittle — the #1 storm-limb dropper in the parish.
  • Bald cypressDown in the wet ground; access and timing matter most.
  • PineCommon in newer subdivisions; snaps and uproots in high wind.

What areas do you serve around Houma?

All of Houma and Terrebonne Parish, plus nearby Thibodaux, Bayou Cane, Gray, Schriever, and Morgan City. If you’re not sure whether you’re in range, just call and ask.

Do you charge for an estimate?

No. Estimates are free and come with no obligation. We look at the tree, the access, and what’s around it, then give you a firm price.

How fast can you come out after a storm?

Storm and emergency work is a priority. After a major hurricane the whole parish is calling at once, so the sooner you reach us the sooner you’re in the queue.

Do you haul away the wood and debris?

Yes, haul-off is included unless you’d rather keep the wood. Some folks want the trunk cut to firewood length and left — just tell us.
