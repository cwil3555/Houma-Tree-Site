## Read before sending

1. **Hoot Reading families: check Sherri's Hoot agreement first.** Tutoring platforms usually ban
   contacting students you met through them (a "non-solicitation" clause), often for 6–12 months after you
   leave. If hers has one, skip Hoot families and use only people she knows personally (homeschool friends,
   church, neighbors, family).
2. **Facebook groups: read each group's rules.** Many ban business posts or allow them only on a set
   "promo day." Ask the admin if unsure. One post per group; reply to every comment the same day.
3. **Outschool: everything stays on Outschool.** Their teacher policy bans sending families to an outside
   website, sharing contact details, or taking payment off the platform. Strikes lead to removal. Don't link
   or mention readingwithsherri.com there. Families who want private lessons book Sherri's 1-on-1 class
   *on* Outschool.
4. **The referral reward is a decision for you and Sherri.** The drafts offer one free 45-min lesson ($65
   value) to the referring family once the new family books a package. Change it or drop it.
5. Every website lead says where it came from in the form's "How did you hear about us?" answer.
   Keep a simple tally per channel.

### 3a. Group class (how families find her)

- **Title:** Learn to Read: Phonics Fun for Beginning Readers (Ages 5–7)
- **Format:** Ongoing, meets once a week, 30 minutes
- **Class size:** 2–4 learners
- **Suggested price:** $14 per learner per class
- **Summary (one line):** Build letter-sound skills, blending, and sight words with a 30-year teacher in a small, cheerful group.
- **Class description:**
  > In this small, ongoing class, beginning readers practice the skills that make reading "click":
  > letter sounds, blending sounds into words, short-vowel words, and common sight words. Each week
  > follows a fun, predictable routine: a sound warm-up, a phonics game, reading real words together,
  > and a short story we read as a group. Learners get lots of chances to read aloud in a supportive
  > setting, and every child gets individual attention because the class stays small.
  >
  > Learners can join any week. Each class reviews earlier sounds while adding a new one.
- **About the teacher:**
  > Hi, I'm Sherri! I've been an educator for more than 30 years. I have a bachelor's degree in Early
  > Childhood Education from Auburn University, K–12 classroom experience, training in EBLI
  > (Evidence-Based Literacy Instruction), experience teaching children with dyslexia, 2 years as an
  > online reading tutor, and I homeschooled my own children. Watching a child realize "I can read this!" is my favorite part of
  > teaching.
- **Learning goals:** Identify letter sounds; blend 3-letter words; read common sight words; build confidence reading aloud.
- **Supplies:** Paper, pencil, and a few crayons. Optional: a small whiteboard and marker.
- **Parental guidance:** None needed. Parents of 5-year-olds may want to stay nearby for the first class.
