# Tutoring marketplaces: are they worth Sherri's time? (2026-10-25)

Free/local channels (her own network, homeschool Facebook groups) come first: no fees, full price,
leads land on readingwithsherri.com. This compares the paid marketplaces as the *second* channel.

| | **Outschool** | **Wyzant** | **Varsity Tutors** |
|---|---|---|---|
| What it is | Kids' class marketplace (group + 1-on-1) | Tutor marketplace: parents search, message, book | Tutoring company: assigns students to tutors |
| Who sets price | Sherri | Sherri | Varsity Tutors |
| Platform cut / pay | 30% of class price (official policy) | ~25% of her rate (third-party sources; unconfirmed on Wyzant's own page) | Pay set by them: job posts show ~$18–40/hr for reading roles |
| Sherri's take at her $65/45-min rate | ~$45.50 (or price at $70 → $49) | ~$49 | n/a (they set it) |
| How families find her | Browse by age/subject; group classes act as a cheap "first taste" | Search "reading tutor"; her profile, reviews, and response speed rank her | They match students to her; no profile to build |
| Off-platform rules | Strict: no contact info, links, or outside payment; strikes → removal | All payments must go through Wyzant; contact info OK once a student has payment on file | Students belong to Varsity Tutors |
| Builds her own brand? | Partly (reviews, followers on Outschool) | Partly (reviews on Wyzant) | No (like Hoot) |
| Startup effort | Apply + background check, write listings, plan a repeatable group class | Free profile + background check, write profile, answer inquiries fast | Apply, interview/tests, then take assigned sessions |

## Verdict

1. **Outschool: worth it (best second channel).** Built for kids and full of homeschool families, so it's the
   closest match to her strengths. Families can find her with no following, and a cheap group class turns into
   1-on-1 bookings. Downsides: 30% cut, and those students can never move to the website.
2. **Wyzant: worth a profile, lower priority.** Parents there are already shopping for a private tutor
   (strong intent), and she keeps her own pricing. Downside: crowded, and new tutors with no reviews get
   few inquiries until they win a few. Low effort to keep a profile up alongside Outschool.
3. **Varsity Tutors: skip.** It's a tutoring *job* (much like Hoot): they set the pay (~$18–40/hr), own the
   students, and nothing builds toward her own business. Only worth it if she wants guaranteed hours fast.

**Suggested order:** warm network + Facebook (now) → Outschool (apply now; approval takes time) → Wyzant
profile once Outschool is running. Re-check after 60 days using the form's "How did you hear about us?"
tally plus each platform's booking counts.

## Verify on the platform before signing up
- Wyzant's exact current commission (and whether it really drops for repeat students / is 0% for students
  she brings) — sources were third-party blogs; Wyzant's fee help page returned 404.
- Varsity Tutors' current reading-tutor pay; job-board ranges can be stale.
