About us

Local tree work, done the right way

Houma Tree Pros is built around one idea: Terrebonne Parish homeowners should be able to get a tree handled by a local crew, with a straight price and a clean site, without chasing anybody down.

Built for Houma, not a national chain

Plenty of the names that show up when you search "tree removal near me" aren’t local at all — they’re booking services that farm your call out to whoever’s available. When you call us, you’re reaching people who know the parish, the trees, and what the ground does after a week of rain.

That local knowledge matters here more than most places. South Louisiana trees grow in soft, wet ground, on tight lots, right up against houses and lines — and every storm season puts them to the test. Doing this work well takes crews who’ve done it here, not a template and a truck.

How we work

Straight pricing

Free on-site estimates and a firm number before we start. No bait prices, no surprises when the work is done.

Careful on site

Rigging and rope work to bring trees down in sections, so nothing lands on your roof, your fence, or your neighbor’s yard.

Clean when we leave

We haul off the wood and debris and leave the site tidy — or leave you firewood and mulch if that’s what you want.
