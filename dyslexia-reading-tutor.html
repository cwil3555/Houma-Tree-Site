Signs your child may need reading help

  • Guesses words from the first letter or the picture
  • Reads slowly and loses the meaning
  • Spells the same word different ways
  • Avoids reading or says it's "boring"
  • Is behind classmates even with extra practice

How lessons help

Sherri is trained in EBLI (Evidence-Based Literacy Instruction), a structured, explicit approach that teaches the sounds of English and how they're written, step by step, so children decode words instead of guessing. Each lesson reviews what your child knows, teaches one new skill directly, practices it together, and ends with real reading at the right level.

Lessons stay short, encouraging and one-on-one, so your child gets a teacher's full attention every minute.

What lessons are not

Lessons are tutoring, not a medical or psychological evaluation, and they don't diagnose dyslexia. If you're unsure, the free assessment is a good first step, and your pediatrician or school can advise on formal testing.
