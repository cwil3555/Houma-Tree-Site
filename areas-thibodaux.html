What tree work is like in Thibodaux

Thibodaux grew up along Bayou Lafourche, and the ground shows it. The soil holds water, roots sit shallow, and a tree that looks solid can be one soaked week away from leaning. A lot of the removals we run here are not dead trees at all — they are healthy-looking ones that have started to tip over soft ground toward a house or a fence line. If a tree of yours has started leaning since the last heavy rain, it is worth a look before the next storm rather than after.

The older streets around downtown and Nicholls State are lined with live oaks and pecans that have stood for generations. They are the best thing about those neighborhoods and the hardest to work around. Big canopies over rooftops, power drops, and tight driveways mean we rarely just fell a tree here — a climber sets ropes and we lower it in controlled sections so nothing lands where it should not. That is slower and more skilled than a cut-and-drop, and it is the difference between a clean job and a new hole in your roof.

Thibodaux is Lafourche Parish, and that can matter

Houma is Terrebonne Parish; Thibodaux is Lafourche. For a tree in your own yard it makes no difference — you own it, we take it down. But removals near a public right-of-way, a drainage servitude, or a protected oak can need sign-off from the City of Thibodaux or the parish first. We have run enough of these to spot when a job needs a permit, and we will tell you before we start, not after.

Storm and hurricane cleanup

This stretch of Louisiana takes the brunt of it. Ida came through in 2021 and left Lafourche and Terrebonne buried in downed and hung-up trees for months. When the next storm hits, the trees that fail are almost always the ones that were already weak — a water oak with a hollow trunk, a pine with a lean. We handle storm removals across Thibodaux and can usually reach a tree on your house or blocking your drive faster than the out-of-town crews that flood in after a hurricane. If it is an active emergency, call and we will tell you honestly where you stand in line.

Thibodaux tree service questions

Do you actually cover Thibodaux, or just Houma?

Yes, Thibodaux is a regular run for us. We are based in Houma, about twenty minutes down Highway 24, and we bring the same crews and the same straight pricing up to Thibodaux and the rest of Lafourche Parish.

How much does tree removal cost in Thibodaux?

Most residential removals run about $300 to $1,500, with the average around $500. Price comes down to the tree’s height, its trunk size, and how close it stands to your house or power lines. Emergency and storm work runs higher because of the added risk. The only real number comes from a free on-site look.

Do I need a permit to remove a tree in Thibodaux?

For most trees in your own yard, no. Removals near a public right-of-way, a drainage servitude, or certain protected oaks can require sign-off from the City of Thibodaux or Lafourche Parish. If your job is one of those, we will flag it before we start.

Can you grind the stump too?

Yes. Stump grinding uses different equipment, so it is a separate service, but we can handle the removal and the grinding in one visit — and bundling the two is usually cheaper than booking two trips.
