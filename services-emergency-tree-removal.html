Emergency & Storm — questions

Do you do emergency tree removal after hurricanes?

Yes — storm and hurricane response is a core part of what we do in Houma. After a major storm, demand is high across the whole parish, so calling early gets you in the queue sooner.

A tree fell on my house — what should I do first?

If anyone is hurt or a power line is involved, call 911 and the utility first and stay away from the tree. Then call us. Don’t climb on or try to cut a tree that’s under load — it can shift and snap back with enormous force.

Will insurance cover emergency removal?

Often, yes — especially when a tree hits a covered structure. We can’t speak for your policy, but we can document the work with photos so you have what you need to file. Check your homeowner’s coverage.
